The dbc files related to the QC-CAN bus have been updated between May 25th 2026 and Jun 1st 2026 
to correct the StateOfCharge (SOC%) and change all Motorola (big-endian) to Intel (little-endian)
for 16-bit data values.  The scaling applied to the 16-bit values has also been changed from 0.01
to 1, which seems to be correct for the new little-endian format (from old log examples).

These changes have now been applied to the expanded CAN-IDs in file CAN_ID_100-109.txt


Note re CAN ‘chargeSequence’ codes 0x20, 0x26, 0x29, 0x31, 0x37, 0x38 :-

These codes (which seem not be used in the latest ZE1+ Leaf) are/were used to indicate the current charging
sequence state(s) of the EV and EVSE, and provide a software ‘handshake’ between the charger and car.
The analogue handshakes on CHAdeMO pin 2 (signal d1/SS1 - Charge Enable from EVSE to EV), Charge Permit on 
CHAdeMO pin 4 (signal ‘k’ from EV to EVSE), and Charge Signal 2 (d2/SS2) on pin 10 – enable EV contactors) 
seem instead to be an adequate interlock, and are the CHAdeMO standard, whereas the CAN chargeSequenceCodes
are manufacturer-specific (Nissan original use), and may differ for other car makes
